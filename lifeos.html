L LifeOS

A private personal assistant.

LifeOS runs on its owner’s own computer. It can work with Gmail and other Google services only after the owner explicitly grants access.

Built around user control

  • Access is requested only for services the owner chooses to connect.
  • Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account.
  • Credentials and assistant data remain on the owner’s device.
  • Google user data is never sold or used for advertising.
