Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

About LifeOS

LifeOS is a privately operated personal assistant that runs on its owner’s computer. This policy explains how LifeOS handles information obtained through Google APIs.

Google data LifeOS accesses

After the owner grants permission, LifeOS may access the Google Account identifier, email address, Gmail messages, message metadata, labels, drafts, and related mailbox information. LifeOS requests only the access needed for features the owner chooses to use.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features requested by the owner, such as searching mail, organizing messages, preparing drafts, and displaying relevant information. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build advertising profiles.

Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner’s computer with owner-only file permissions. LifeOS does not operate a central database containing the owner’s Gmail content.

AI processing

Some requested features may send selected content to the AI inference provider configured by the owner. This happens only to perform the requested task and is subject to the selected provider’s data-handling terms. LifeOS does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Sharing

LifeOS does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising or unrelated purposes. Data may be transmitted to Google to provide the requested service, or to a configured AI provider when required for a feature initiated by the owner. LifeOS may disclose information when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored credentials and assistant data remain until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke LifeOS’s Google access at Google Account permissions and can remove credentials and related local data from the LifeOS installation.

Security

Access tokens and local data are kept on the owner’s device with restricted permissions. The owner is responsible for securing the device account and following the revocation procedures above.

Google API Limited Use

LifeOS’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the application.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when LifeOS’s data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, use the support address shown on the LifeOS Google authorization screen or contact the developer through GitHub.
